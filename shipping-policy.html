SHIPPING POLICY

01Order Processing

Orders are typically processed within 1–3 business days after order confirmation and successful payment. Orders placed on weekends or public holidays will be processed on the next working business day. In certain circumstances, processing may take longer due to product availability or logistical delays.

02Shipping Coverage

Dentessa currently ships products to locations within India. Availability of shipping to specific regions may depend on courier service coverage.

For international shipping enquiries, please contact us at atyantainc@gmail.com.

03Shipping Methods

Orders are shipped through reliable third-party courier and logistics partners. The choice of carrier may vary depending on:

  • Delivery location
  • Product type
  • Order size and weight

04Estimated Delivery Time

Typical delivery timelines:

  • Metro cities: 2–5 business days
  • Non-metro cities and towns: 4–7 business days
  • Remote locations: 5–10 business days
Delivery timelines are estimates and may be affected by courier delays, weather conditions, or other factors beyond our control.

05Shipping Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, will be displayed at checkout before the order is confirmed. Dentessa may offer free shipping promotions on certain orders or during promotional campaigns.

06Order Tracking

Once an order is shipped, customers may receive shipping confirmation and tracking details, allowing them to monitor the delivery status of their shipment.

07Damaged or Lost Shipments

If a shipment arrives damaged, incomplete, or appears tampered with, customers are requested to:

  • Notify us immediately upon receipt
  • Provide photographs of the package and products
  • Retain the original packaging for verification

Dentessa will review such cases and provide appropriate assistance.

08Delivery Address Accuracy

Customers are responsible for providing accurate shipping information, including complete address details and contact numbers. Dentessa will not be responsible for delays or failed deliveries resulting from incorrect or incomplete address information.

09Shipping Delays

Dentessa shall not be held responsible for delays caused by:

  • Courier service disruptions
  • Natural events or weather conditions
  • Government regulations or restrictions
  • Other circumstances beyond our control
