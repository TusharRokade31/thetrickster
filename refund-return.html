Returns & Refunds

RETURN & REFUND POLICY

01General Policy

Due to the nature of dental materials and clinical products, Dentessa maintains strict quality and safety standards for all items supplied. Returns or refunds may be considered only under specific circumstances as outlined in this policy.

02Eligibility for Returns

Customers may request a return under the following conditions:

  • The product received is damaged during transit
  • The product received is defective
  • The incorrect product was delivered
  • The product is missing from the shipment
Return requests must be submitted within 48 hours of receiving the order.

03Condition of Returned Products

To be eligible for return, products must meet all of the following:

  • Unused and unopened
  • Original packaging remains intact
  • All labels, seals, and accessories are included
  • Returned in saleable condition
For safety and regulatory reasons, opened or used dental materials cannot be returned or refunded.

04Return Request Procedure

Customers requesting a return should contact Dentessa with the following:

  • Order number
  • Product name
  • Description of the issue
  • Photographs showing the product and packaging

Once the request is reviewed and approved, instructions for return shipment will be provided.

05Refund Process

Once the returned product is received and inspected, Dentessa will notify the customer regarding approval or rejection of the refund. If approved, the refund will be processed using the original payment method, or an alternative method agreed upon with the customer. Refund processing times may vary depending on the payment provider.

06Non-Returnable Items

The following items are generally not eligible for return or refund:

  • Products that have been opened or used
  • Products with tampered seals
  • Products returned without original packaging
  • Products damaged due to improper storage or handling after delivery

07Shipping Costs for Returns

Dentessa will cover the return shipping cost if the return is due to:

  • Incorrect shipment
  • Defective product
  • Damage during transit

For other approved return situations, the customer may be responsible for return shipping charges.

08Policy Modifications

Dentessa reserves the right to modify or update this Return & Refund Policy at any time without prior notice. Updated versions will be published on this page.
